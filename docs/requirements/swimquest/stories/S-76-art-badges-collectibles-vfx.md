# S-76: Art: badges, collectibles, marine life and effects

- **Priority:** P1
- **Covers:** FR-61, FR-82 · **Decisions:** Q-15, Q-19, Q-47, Q-52
- **Depends on stories:** S-18

## User story
As the stakeholder, I want the 8 badges, collectibles, marine life and effects delivered, so that rewards and the map look rewarding.

## Acceptance criteria
1. **Eight badges**
   - Given the delivered art
   - When badges are shown
   - Then Participation, Explorer, Perseverance, Teamwork, Conqueror, Season, Special and Lý Sơn each have their own image without embedded text, plus the "HOÀN THÀNH" emblem
2. **Collectibles**
   - Given the delivered art
   - When collectibles are shown
   - Then map fragments, treasure chest (closed and opened), pearl, starfish, coin and energy orb each have an image
3. **Marine life and decoration**
   - Given the map
   - When it is shown
   - Then marine life and the star, shell and message bottle appear as decoration only
4. **Effects**
   - Given a milestone, finish or chest opening happens
   - When its moment is shown
   - Then a simple sparkle or glow effect plays
5. **Listed for replacement**
   - Given these assets
   - When the stakeholder opens the replacement list (S-77)
   - Then each is listed with its source and spec

## Out of scope
- Award rules (S-48 to S-50, S-83, S-84).
