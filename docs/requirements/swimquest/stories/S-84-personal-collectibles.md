# S-84: Personal collectibles: pearls, starfish, coins and the energy orb

- **Priority:** P2
- **Covers:** FR-82 · **Decisions:** Q-16, Q-52
- **Depends on stories:** S-21, S-20, S-76

## User story
As a player, I want small collectibles for my own swimming, so that each good week and long swim leaves something to keep.

## Acceptance criteria
1. **Pearl**
   - Given I had 3 or more active days in a week
   - When the week ends
   - Then I get a pearl in that tournament
2. **Starfish**
   - Given a swim earned me a long-swim bonus
   - When it counts
   - Then I get a starfish
3. **Coins**
   - Given my verified km counting in the tournament
   - When they are counted
   - Then I have one coin per full km
4. **Energy orb**
   - Given my weekly multiplier is active this week (2+ active days)
   - When my swimmer is shown on the map or team page
   - Then it shows the energy orb; the orb is not collected
5. **Shown and removed**
   - Given I have collectibles
   - When I open my profile or the team page
   - Then I see them per tournament; if the swim behind one is revoked, it is removed

## Out of scope
- Team collectibles (S-83).
