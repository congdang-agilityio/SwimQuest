# S-82: Share the team's finish

- **Priority:** P2
- **Covers:** FR-42, FR-16 · **Decisions:** Q-51
- **Depends on stories:** S-19, S-56

## User story
As a team member, I want to share a picture and a link of our finish at Lý Sơn, so that friends see what we achieved.

## Acceptance criteria
1. **Way in**
   - Given my team has finished
   - When I open the finish experience or Voyage
   - Then I see "Share achievement"
2. **Picture**
   - Given I choose "Share achievement"
   - When the picture is made
   - Then it shows team name and emblem, route, finish date and team total, and I can save it
3. **Public finish page**
   - Given the share link
   - When anyone opens it, signed in or not
   - Then they see a page with the same content
4. **Phone share menu**
   - Given I choose to share
   - When the phone's share menu opens
   - Then I can send the picture or link through any app (Zalo, Facebook, Messenger, ...)
5. **Privacy respected**
   - Given teammates set their profile to private
   - When the picture or public page is shown
   - Then their personal details appear only as their privacy settings allow

## Out of scope
- Sharing other achievements (not required).

## Notes
- If the finish is withdrawn, the public page shows that the team is no longer at Lý Sơn (BA reading, BR-32).
